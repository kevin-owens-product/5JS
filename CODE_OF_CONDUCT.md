# Community conduct

5js welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels. Treat people with respect, explain technical disagreements with evidence, and help keep discussion focused on the project.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not use issues or pull requests for spam or personal attacks.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community. Responses should reflect the severity and recurrence of the behavior. These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces.

Report abusive content through GitHub's **Report content** option. For non-sensitive moderation concerns, open an issue describing the behavior without repeating personal information. Do not post private details or allegations publicly; use GitHub's private abuse-reporting process for those reports. A separate project-specific confidential contact has not been established for this alpha.
